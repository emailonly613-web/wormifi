The five-minute mission
  1. Play one live run. The human count is shown honestly; labeled AI fills empty seats while this first cohort forms.
  2. Judge the feel. Steering, treasure readability, body smoothness, mobile space, and whether dying makes you want another run are the real test.
  3. Invite only if it earns it. Start a free 10, 20, or 30-player Captain Room and send the copied link. Friends land in that exact arena as guests.

What this proves

Not scale, revenue, or virality. It proves whether 50 outside people can arrive, understand the loop, complete a run, and voluntarily create the first friend-to-friend chain.

Privacy stays optional

Wormifi asks before using anonymous gameplay analytics. Saying no does not block the game, private rooms, or sharing. If you allow it, campaign labels and play events help the team see which paths produced real runs—never your arena name, room code, or full invite URL.
