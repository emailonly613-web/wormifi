The shipping record

What shipped, and when

The public record, newest first. If it's on this page, you can touch it in the game today; the roadmap holds what's still in the workshop.

July 2026

  • Turbo, made visible. Sprint reserve now shows inside your worm, and the button states the price: 2.0× speed, −4 size per second.
  • Living skin materials. All twelve themes gained animated materials with per-device motion and glow controls in the Skin Studio, plus standard/RARE/LEGEND complexity tiers.
  • Slower spatial play. Room paces arrived — Harbor (patient default), Classic, and Tempest — alongside durable rooms and the pirate-powers relic work.
  • The Black Pearl Relay. A second board: two wrap-and-charge capstan objectives, Port and Starboard, for tactical crews.
  • Install without an app store. A signed Android package downloadable from the site, iPhone home-screen install, desktop install — with offline solo play.
  • The guide library and devlog. Strategy, genre roundups, and this building-in-public record.
  • Site foundations. Real 404s, analytics that respects consent and Do-Not-Track, faster indexing plumbing, and a press kit.
  • Wormifi launches. The living-chain arena goes public: solo rush/endless/practice with exact six-second replays, the live multiplayer preview with labeled AI, friend rooms by code, and the private Photo Skin studio.

How to read this

Dates are when things reached the public game at wormifi.com — the version stamp in the game's corner updates itself, so you're always reading the page that matches the build. Plans live on the roadmap until they earn a line here.
