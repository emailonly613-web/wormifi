Head to head

Wormifi vs Wormate, honestly

Wormate answered the genre's patience problem with sugar: more pickups, faster growth, shorter fuses. We answered it differently. Here's the fair comparison — we make one of these.

What Wormate gets right

Tempo and generosity. Wormate's candy-strewn arenas, growth potions and power-ups make every run feel eventful from the first second — it's the snack-food version of the genre in the best sense, and for quick sessions or younger players its energy is genuinely hard to beat. It has years of audience and iteration behind it.

The design fork: power-ups versus symmetry

Wormate's power-ups make runs swingy on purpose — potions and boosts create moments where one worm simply has more than another. Wormifi's bet is symmetry: every captain in a room plays under identical rules forever — same speeds, same sprint price (2.0× for −4 size/second, dropped as loot behind you), same treasure physics. Our variety lives in the room settings instead: two boards and three paces, chosen per room, applying to everyone equally.

Matching Wormate's energy in Wormifi

If Wormate's speed is the draw, set a room to Tempest — high pressure, less reaction time — or run the 90-second solo rush. You get the arcade fuse with the fairness rules intact, no ads between runs, and friend rooms by code when you want your chaos witnessed. Both games install on phones; ours skips the app store and keeps solo play working offline.

The quick verdict

Wormate for maximum sugar and swing. Wormifi for speed you opt into on a level field, costs you can see, and rooms that never dress bots as people. Related: more games with Wormate's tempo.
