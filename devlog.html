Why write this in public

Worm games are a genre where every developer quietly copies everyone else. Writing our decisions down keeps us honest about which ideas are ours, which are inherited, and why each one is in the game. It also means that when the game claims something — fair collisions, honest sprint costs — you can read exactly how it works underneath.
