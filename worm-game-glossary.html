The worm arena glossary

Eighteen terms that cover everything that happens in a worm arena — from the four ways to die to the words on Wormifi's own boards.

The terms

Arena
The bounded playfield every worm shares. Wormifi's arenas are boards: Open Seas and the Black Pearl Relay.
Bait
Placing your body near dropped mass so a greedy rival's head meets it. The genre's oldest trap.
Boost / Sprint
Trading a resource for speed. In Wormifi: 2.0x speed for 4 size per second, dropped behind you as wake loot.
Capstan
A wrap-and-charge landmark on the Black Pearl Relay board. Wrapping it charges the objective; two exist, Port and Starboard.
Coil
Fully encircling a smaller worm and tightening until it has nowhere left. Famous, slow, and frequently vultured.
Conserved mass
The design law that killed crews and spent sprint drop as real collectable mass instead of vanishing.
Crew
Wormifi's word for your body segments — the living chain behind your head.
Cut
Making a rival's head strike a body. The genre's only verb that matters.
Head
The only vulnerable point of any worm. Everything in the genre is geometry around protecting yours and ambushing theirs.
Hoard
The marked treasure a cut rival drops where it died. Rich, and dangerous to collect — the cutter is usually nearby.
Labeled AI
Bot players wearing an explicit AI tag, so a room's population is never overstated.
Pace
A room-wide speed setting. Wormifi ships Harbor (patient), Classic, and Tempest (high pressure).
Pinch
Closing the open side on a rival whose other exits are already blocked by walls, giants, or your body.
Relic
A time-limited specialist pickup. Example: the Loot Compass, which pulls nearby neutral treasure and your own wake loot.
Server authority
The multiplayer model where one server decides all collisions and every browser renders its verdict.
Snapshot
One server update of the whole room's state. Wormifi's live rooms run 15 per second.
Vulture
Collecting a hoard you didn't earn while the two who fought over it are still untangling.
Wake loot
The trail of spent mass a sprinting worm leaves behind, collectable by anyone, coloured by who paid for it.
