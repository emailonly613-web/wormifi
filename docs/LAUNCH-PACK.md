# Wormifi launch pack

Everything needed to get Wormifi listed and talked about, written to be pasted
without editing. Nothing here needs to be rewritten or adapted per platform —
the character limits are already respected.

**The problem this solves:** Google currently reads "wormifi" as a misspelling
and offers Wiimmfi, Wi-Fi worms and UniFi instead. That is not a ranking
problem, it is an *entity* problem — Google has no record of what the word
means, so it substitutes the nearest word it knows. It is fixed by independent
sites using the word to mean the game, consistently, in enough places that the
association becomes the obvious one. Every listing below is one more vote.

### Hacker News (Show HN)

Post between 8–10am ET on a weekday. Title must start with "Show HN:".

> Show HN: Wormifi – A browser arena game where all reward is conserved mass

First comment (post immediately after):
> Author here. The design constraint I set myself was that nothing in the arena appears from nowhere. Cut a rival and their mass becomes loot on the floor rather than a score bonus. Sprint and the size you spend drops behind you as a trail in your colour that anyone can collect. The consequence I liked is that a large lead is never abstract — it's physically present and always takeable.
>
> Live rooms are server-authoritative: the browser sends intent and renders what it's told, and never decides who was cut. You see correction on a bad connection, which is the honest price of one authority instead of several convenient ones.
>
> No account, no download, no purchases. Installs as a PWA on iOS and as a signed 1.9 MB package on Android, both without an app store.

### TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Reels hooks

Record 8–15 seconds of actual gameplay, hook on screen in the first second:

- "This game punishes you for running away" *(show sprint dropping loot, rival collecting it)*
- "The bigger you get the more you can kill yourself" *(show a self-collision)*
- "Free. No download. No app store." *(show it launching from a home screen icon)*
- "Everything you kill drops what it was carrying" *(show a cut and the hoard)*
- "Play it in 3 seconds" *(tap link → playing, unedited)*

Caption every one with `wormifi.com` in plain text, not just the bio link —
the spelled-out word in captions is exactly what teaches search engines the
term.
