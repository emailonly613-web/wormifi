# Wormifi 2026 Product Goal and Category Baseline Contract

Status: **OWNER-DIRECTED PRODUCT CONTRACT**
Effective: 2026-07-30

## The official goal

Wormate is a proven 2016 category reference for where Wormifi must **start**, not
where Wormifi is allowed to finish. Wormifi begins in 2026 with modern product,
AI-assisted creation, automated verification and live-service tooling. The goal
is therefore not a small improvement over the old category. The goal is a
generation-ahead multiplayer worm game whose first seconds feel immediately
familiar and satisfying, while its identity, customization, social play and
strategic systems make the older baseline feel incomplete.

“Generation ahead” is a product target, not an advertising claim. It becomes a
claim only after external players and reproducible public evidence support it.

## Non-negotiable body and growth rule

1. The starting player is a **small, short, plump, continuous worm** with a
   complete head, body and tail. Small never means starving, stringy, skeletal,
   bead-like or chain-like.
2. The skin reads as one continuous surface flowing across the worm. Decorative
   characters, portraits or markings may live on that surface, but may not turn
   each body section into a disconnected token.
3. Eating must create an unmistakable build-up. Early growth visibly increases
   length and presence; sustained growth also increases girth. A player must be
   able to feel the contrast between the compact spawn and the powerful worm it
   becomes without reading a number.
4. The authoritative collision shape and the visible body must agree. A fuller
   visual body may not hide a smaller lethal collider, and a large collider may
   not extend invisibly outside the rendered skin.
5. Owner review of native phone and desktop captures is required. Passing a
   geometry, frame-rate or screenshot-existence assertion cannot override a
   human verdict that the worm still looks thin or chain-like.
6. Live motion must preserve the broad open-zone, readable pickup-density,
   multi-creature camera and smooth close-pass grammar in
   `SPATIAL-MOTION-FEEL-CONTRACT.md`, while artwork and presentation remain
   original to Wormifi.
7. A defeated creature becomes a deterministic head-to-tail treasure trace
   following its final body polyline. Rival Echoes never explode into a random
   cloud around the head.
8. Ordinary food advances the long-run growth curve in small increments under
   `GROWTH-PACING-CONTRACT.md`; enormous size is earned over a sustained run,
   while chests, rival hoards and disclosed multipliers provide acceleration.
9. Touch-capable portrait viewports never enter supported gameplay. Mobile runs
   start and continue in landscape under `MOBILE-LANDSCAPE-PLAY-CONTRACT.md`.
10. Quality must remain perceptibly consistent through a real long session.
    One polished opening minute cannot hide body seams, detached effects,
    density collapse, input drift, thermal slowdown or reconnect degradation
    that appears later. The exact release candidate needs an uninterrupted
    four-hour human/device quality review in addition to automated room soaks.

## The category starting line we keep

These are accepted category behaviors. Wormifi should offer them at least as
clearly and reliably while using original code, art, names, maps, sounds and UI.

- instant guest play with real multiplayer;
- intuitive pointer and touch steering with a deliberate speed boost;
- a small worm that grows steadily and visibly by eating abundant, readable
  objects and defeated-rival remains;
- one simple lethal law: a head that strikes another worm's body loses;
- fair opportunities for a small player to defeat a large player;
- a readable leaderboard, world position/radar and room identity;
- expressive cosmetic skins available without changing competitive power;
- clear, temporary power-ups whose effects can be learned at a glance;
- stable desktop and landscape-mobile play, reconnect behavior and social play with
  friends.

## Newcomer pace and reaction rule

- The launcher must offer Harbor, Classic and Tempest pace profiles before a
  run. Harbor is the patient 100-unit/second default; Classic preserves the
  prior 212-unit/second pace as an opt-in fast room and Tempest remains the
  deliberate 235-unit/second high-pressure option. The exact values and room rules live in
  `PIRATE-POWER-PARITY-CONTRACT.md`.
- Live pace is a visible room-wide server rule, never a private per-player
  advantage. Solo and Practice may use the captain's local choice. Challenges
  and new replays bind pace; legacy replays mean Classic.
- Sprint remains a clear, deliberate acceleration rather than becoming the
  ordinary travel pace at any selected speed.
- The opening seconds must give a first-time player enough reaction time to
  recognize the head, turn once and understand nearby danger before ordinary
  lethal exposure. A slower base pace complements, but does not replace, fair
  spawn placement and newcomer shielding.
- This is a feel target, not a license to make steering sluggish. Accept only a
  value that preserves immediate pointer/touch response, fair human-versus-bot
  movement and satisfying treasure collection in owner device testing.

This is a capability reference, not permission to copy Wormate assets, layouts,
text, maps, code or distinctive visual designs.

## The generation-ahead Wormifi destination

Wormifi must combine the category starting line with a connected moat that the
older baseline does not provide:

### 1. A skin studio, not a skin picker

- Multiple self/family photos can become an ordered, previewable worm skin.
- Crop, reorder, remove, consent, privacy, deletion and visibility are explicit.
- The photo treatment follows the continuous body instead of producing a row of
  unrelated circular faces.
- Original authored skins remain rich enough that players who use no photos do
  not receive a second-class worm.

### 2. Pirate treasure, not anonymous food dots

- Ordinary pickups are unmistakable cut treasure at actual play scale.
- High-value chests, defeated-rival hoards and Relics have different silhouettes
  before their glow or color is considered.
- The pirate world, feedback and collection rhythm feel authored rather than
  reskinned pellets.

### 3. Strategic world play

- Selected boards contain authoritative charging stations that reward a risky
  wrap while exposing the player to encirclement and interruption.
- A useful original pirate-chart radar shows bounds, heading, relevant
  landmarks and deliberately balanced public events.
- Fair Relics add route and timing decisions without pay-to-win, hostile input
  control, hidden immunity or unreadable collision exceptions.
- The required launch power set covers temporary attraction, speed, any-angle
  quick turning, camera pullback and bounded x2/x3/x5 neutral-treasure growth,
  all redesigned as disclosed pirate powers under
  `PIRATE-POWER-PARITY-CONTRACT.md`.

### 4. Social multiplayer that is a product, not a socket demo

- A visible room number persists through lobby, play, help, death, result,
  replay, invite and reconnect states.
- Creating, sharing and joining a friend room is an obvious first-class journey.
- Named humans can reproduce synchronized movement, collision, death, loot,
  score and reconnect behavior on the deployed public build.
- Room ownership, isolation, overload, restart and multi-instance behavior are
  proven before scale language is used.

### 5. A proof advantage

- Deterministic core tests, browser tests, network impairment, load, visual and
  accessibility evidence are run against the same versioned candidate.
- Native phone and desktop captures accompany machine results.
- External human comprehension, fun, retry, return and friend-invite evidence
  is kept separate from bots and automated clients.
- Failed gates stay visible. Evidence is not rewritten to make a claim pass.

## Moat definition

The moat is not “more features.” It is the connected system:

**instantly satisfying worm growth + uniquely personal skins + pirate treasure
and territory strategy + frictionless friend rooms + authoritative public proof**

If any one of those pillars is only a menu label, placeholder, localhost demo or
unverified claim, the moat is not finished.

## Acceptance gates

The product candidate is not ready for the separate SEO lane until all of the
following are true:

1. Fresh spawn, early growth and large-worm captures visibly demonstrate a
   short/plump-to-long/powerful progression on phone and desktop.
2. First-time viewers identify the player as one worm, not a chain, and identify
   ordinary treasure, chests, rival hoards and Relics without coaching.
3. Multi-photo skins work end to end with the declared privacy and visibility
   contract and do not alter collision or competitive power.
4. Charging stations, all approved pirate powers, the three room-visible pace
   profiles, room identity, radar and the full friend-room journey work in
   local tests and deployed multiplayer.
5. The finished feature candidate passes unit, integration, browser,
   accessibility, reconnect, room-isolation, impairment and bounded-load gates.
6. The exact commit/build under test is deployed and rechecked on the public
   domain with named humans on separate devices.
7. External-player evidence supports the permitted product language. Automated
   proof alone cannot establish fun, preference, virality or a generational lead.
8. One real tester completes a four-hour session on the exact candidate while
   periodic captures and telemetry confirm stable controls, body/effect quality,
   density, frame pacing, memory, temperature behavior and room continuity.

## Ownership and execution order

1. This lane finishes the body/growth baseline and the connected moat.
2. This lane runs correctness, multiplayer, failure, load, visual and public
   proof on the finished candidate.
3. The separate SEO session owns SEO implementation and receives only the
   evidence-backed claim packet from this lane.
4. Smoothing/optimization follows the owner's stated ordering and is performed
   against the accepted feature-complete candidate, never against rejected
   placeholders.
5. Stripe remains last unless the owner explicitly changes that order.
