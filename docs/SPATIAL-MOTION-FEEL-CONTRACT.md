# Wormifi Spatial and Motion Feel Contract

Status: **OWNER-REQUIRED FEEL TARGET — DEVICE ACCEPTANCE PENDING**

Owner clarification date: **2026-07-30**

Wormifi must preserve its original pirate world, artwork, names and tuning while
meeting the proven category's readable in-motion spatial grammar. This is a
feel and gameplay contract, not permission to copy another game's assets,
skins, floor, interface, sounds, code or distinctive layouts.

## Live arena grammar

- The playfield reads as broad, open water rather than a small boxed level or a
  busy decorative scene. The floor supports motion contrast and never competes
  with heads, solid bodies, treasure or powers.
- Ordinary treasure is distributed continuously enough to make routing and
  growth immediately legible, but leaves navigable water between clusters.
- The ordinary camera can hold the local captain plus several nearby long or
  enormous creatures during close passes. The local head remains findable and
  the lethal body edge remains readable at actual desktop and landscape-phone scale.
- Growth visibly changes a creature from short and plump to long and powerful.
  Large creatures remain one smooth, continuous living body rather than a row
  of discs, flat tube or circular head attached to tokens.
- Pointer and touch steering respond immediately. The body follows in a smooth
  continuous curve, close passes feel deliberate, and large-creature turns do
  not kink, teleport or visibly detach.
- Every living head carries Wormifi's original three-crest bow wave: curved
  sea-light, lantern-gold and violet beams ahead of the face make heading,
  turning pressure and close passes readable in motion. The bow wave is a
  presentation layer only. It never extends a hitbox, blocks movement, damages
  another player or promises safe clearance. Reduced-motion mode freezes its
  pulse while retaining the directional shape.
- A server-confirmed sprint adds opposing inset shimmer rails and a brighter
  head-skin pulse. No body point, silhouette or collision circle vibrates.
  Reduced-motion mode freezes the sprint signal into a steady highlight, so
  the gameplay state remains readable without decorative movement.
- Harbor, Classic and Tempest change the room's movement pressure without
  changing this camera, collision or continuous-body truth. Emerald Spyglass
  is the disclosed temporary exception that shows 25% farther.

## Defeated-worm treasure trace

When a creature is defeated, its released Rival Echo treasure must preserve the
creature's final centerline from head through tail:

- Drops are sampled deterministically along the final head/body polyline in
  order. The first and last samples anchor the head and tail when more than one
  drop is produced.
- A curved, hooked or looped creature therefore leaves the same recognizable
  curved, hooked or looped treasure trace. Drops never originate from a radial
  burst, random cloud or unrelated scatter around the head.
- Mass conservation, visible pickup size, Echo-bank limits, pickup rules and
  room caps remain exact. Shape is not a license to duplicate or destroy mass.
- Ordinary collection may naturally open gaps in the trace. Long-room cap
  compaction may merge the oldest Echo history only under the published cap;
  it must still conserve every unit of authoritative mass.
- The server-owned trace and every client snapshot use the same positions.
  Client-only particles may celebrate the defeat but cannot replace, move or
  counterfeit the real collectible path.

## Acceptance

- Deterministic tests prove head-to-tail sampling, curved-path adherence,
  repeatability and exact mass conservation.
- Two live clients observe the same defeated-worm trace and collect the same
  server-owned mass.
- Owner review on desktop and landscape phone confirms the open-zone density, camera,
  close-pass motion and large-creature readability in actual play—not a still
  title screen or synthetic screenshot.
- External players can identify where a defeated creature was and understand
  that its body became collectible treasure without coaching.
