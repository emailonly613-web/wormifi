# Wormifi Long-Run Growth Pacing Contract

Status: **OWNER-REQUIRED CANDIDATE — EXTERNAL RETENTION PROOF PENDING**

Owner clarification date: **2026-07-30**

Wormifi must make enormous size the result of a sustained, skillful run. The
working retention hypothesis is that gradual visible growth preserves a longer
goal horizon, while kills and temporary powers create memorable acceleration.
This is a product hypothesis until external-player retention data supports it.

## Candidate economy

- Ordinary treasure awards 0.2–0.6 size.
- One new body follower requires 30 accumulated size above the starting mass.
- The six-follower starting creature therefore needs 50 maximum-value ordinary
  pickups before gaining its first additional follower.
- Rare treasure chests award 3.5 size. Charging rewards and Rival Echoes remain
  larger strategic gains.
- Treasure Multiplier multiplies every visible positive-mass pickup eaten while
  active at 2×, 3×, 4×, 5× or rare 10×. Stored Echo-bank mass remains conserved; only
  the collected portion receives the multiplier.
- Ordinary pickup density stays satisfying. Slower progression is achieved by
  smaller truthful rewards, not by starving the field or hiding collection.
- Relative to the prior 0.35–1.25 / 20-size candidate, ordinary body-length
  progression is approximately three times slower around the midpoint. This is
  an owner-directed candidate, not a claim that slower automatically retains
  players.

## Acceptance

- Unit tests prove the ordinary range, rare-chest boundary and minimum pickup
  count before the first additional follower.
- Replay and live authority produce the same mass, body length and score.
- Desktop and landscape-phone play confirm growth is smooth rather than jumpy.
- External sessions measure time-to-size milestones, run duration, retries and
  next-day retention before this pacing is called retention-positive.
